# Scale-uniform one-slab defect bridge

This theorem unit identifies the exact quantitative frontier behind the current finite-volume contraction factor.

For the normalized physical one-slab transfer `S_n`, let `F_n` be its full eigenvalue-one subspace and let `R_n` be the restriction of `S_n` to `F_nᗮ`. Let `T_n` denote the corresponding raw finite physical one-slab Wilson transfer.

The unit proves:

1. the exact variational lower bound

   `(1 - ‖R_n‖²) ‖x‖² ≤ ‖x‖² - ‖S_n x‖²`;

2. any normalized squared-defect coefficient `δ ∈ [0,1]` implies

   `δ / 2 ≤ 1 - ‖R_n‖`;

3. any transfer-gap lower bound `ε ≤ 1 - ‖R_n‖` implies the squared-defect coefficient

   `2 ε - ε²`;

4. raw and normalized squared defects are exactly related by the squared raw top-transfer scale `‖T_n‖²`;

5. over a scaling family, existence of a positive scale-independent raw squared-defect coefficient is equivalent to existence of a positive scale-independent lower bound on `1 - ‖R_n‖`.

This unit does **not** assume or claim that the raw inequality holds. It makes the remaining global-gap problem precise: the next model-facing step must prove or refute that raw finite Wilson squared-defect inequality along the physical scaling family.
