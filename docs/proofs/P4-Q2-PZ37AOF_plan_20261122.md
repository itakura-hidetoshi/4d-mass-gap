## Design and Scope

Write b_H(beta)=inner(Omega_(H,beta),v_(H,1)) for the actual unchanged original objects. AOE proves abs(b_H(beta)/beta+1/4)<=81*C_H*beta whenever beta>0 and C_H*beta<=1/2.

For an arbitrary nontrivial filter l, take H:iota->Nat and beta:iota->NNReal. Assume the real product C_(H i)*(beta i) tends to zero and beta is eventually positive. Product convergence makes the half-budget condition eventual, so the absolute normalized slope error is squeezed between0 and81*C_(H i)*(beta i), both tending to zero. The norm-sub criterion then gives slope tending to -1/4.

Define the explicit original-model input beta_H(t)=t/(1+C_H). Nonnegativity of the original budget gives its nonnegative domain proof and C_H*beta_H(t)<=t. This yields an error bound81*t on0<t<=1/2, independent of H. For t tending to zero with eventual positivity, candidate A applies the general product-budget limit; candidate B instead squeezes the explicit81*t bound. They preserve exactly the same public contracts and definition.

The general premise is product convergence, not an assertion that beta itself tends to zero without a separate budget lower bound. H may be arbitrary, including0; no growth/monotonicity premise is added. These are real scalar limits. No vectors from different Hilbert spaces are identified. The explicit beta_H(t) remains volume dependent; no fixed-positive-coupling uniform spectral gap, infinite-volume state, OS reconstruction or continuum Yang-Mills mass gap is claimed.
